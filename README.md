# Understanding Your Money: recorded demos

**Watch them here: https://ajgerdeman.github.io/money-moves-demos/**

Understanding Your Money (the "Money Moves" challenge) is a tool built in one day at the 2026 FSI Veterans Hackathon (Microsoft Garage NYC, November 5, 2026; co-hosted by Microsoft, BlackRock and BNY, with the Bob Woodruff Foundation and Cloud Veterans). A veteran describes a confusing money moment in their own words and adds their documents, such as a paystub, a collection letter or a bank statement. The AI reads the documents, points to the exact line behind every claim, explains it in plain language, asks one question when something is unclear, and suggests one small step for this week plus where to find a real person.

These are recordings of the app running against the live AI, saved after the hackathon's AI access ended.

| Story | Veteran | What's going on |

| `banking` | Marcus Bell, Marine Corps | Three $35 overdraft fees, and a charge he doesn't recognize |
| `credit` | Renee Alvarez, Air Force | Credit card interest jumped after the military rate ended |

Each story has three versions:

- **Replay** (`<story>/<story>.html`): the real app playing back the recorded AI answers, with a moving cursor, typing and captions. You can pause it, speed it up, and explore the page when it ends. It's one self-contained file, so you can also download it and open it offline.
- **Video** (`<story>/<story>.webm`): about 2.5 minutes. Plays in Chrome, Edge, Firefox and Safari.
- **Screenshots** (`<story>/shots/`): one full page per step.

Every person, company, account and document here is made up. No real personal information is used. The tool explains; it never decides. Educational only, not financial, legal or tax advice. Veterans Crisis Line: dial 988, then press 1.
